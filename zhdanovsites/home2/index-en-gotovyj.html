A step-by-step audit

  1. MoneyHow much the site has already missed out on, what it loses each month and what more views and clients would bring
  2. GoogleWhether search engines can see the site, plus simple steps to add it to Google Search Console
  3. TextSpelling, punctuation, typos, consistent style, outdated dates and prices
  4. StructureSite architecture, menus, page logic, duplicate pages
  5. LinksBroken links, redirects, buttons that go nowhere
  6. MobileFirst screen, buttons, readability, pop-ups
  7. SpeedPage weight, images, fonts, unnecessary scripts
  8. SearchTitles, descriptions, structured data, sitemap, map listings
  9. Link previewsHow a shared link looks in WhatsApp, Telegram and social media
  10. EnquiriesForms, online booking, phone numbers, messengers, the customer journey
  11. TrustReviews, prices, contact details, company information
  12. CompliancePrivacy and cookies, AHPRA advertising rules for health, mandatory pages for Russian schools
  13. AccessibilityImage descriptions, contrast, tap target size
  14. CompetitorsWhat they offer that you do not

How we work

From audit to a website that keeps working

First we show what is wrong and what your site could be. Then we pick the format: a fast site we maintain, or a WordPress site where you add news, events and products yourself.

  1. 1. A 14-step audit

    Money, Google, speed, phone layout, legal risks, competitors. Every finding shows what is wrong, why it matters and how to fix it. We also check whether AI assistants can find you.

  2. 2. A concept in 24 hours

    Your new design on a live link that opens on any phone, plus a before-and-after slider with real measurements.

  3. 3. The right format

    Business card or landing page up to 30 pages: a fast site without an admin panel, we make the edits. News, events, catalogue or a large archive: WordPress with our theme and a simple admin panel.

  4. 4. Move and launch

    We put the site on your hosting and domain, move texts, photos and social links, set up analytics and maps, and redirect old addresses.

  5. 5. Updates

    WordPress sites update themselves: new versions of our theme arrive automatically and pages are checked after each update. You add news and photos in the admin panel with a simple guide.
